Our Masters

Learn From Authentic Shaolin Disciples

Every master on our team has trained for at least 15 years. They don't just teach kung fu — they live it.

🥋

Shi Yan Feng

Shaolin Master · 34th Generation

Disciple of Shaolin Temple with over 20 years of training. Specializes in traditional Shaolin forms, Qigong, and Zen meditation. Former performer in the Shaolin Warrior Monks troupe. Known for his patience with beginners and his ability to explain complex movements simply.

💪

Master Li Wei

Sanda Coach · National Champion

Three-time Henan Province Sanda champion. Trained in modern kickboxing and traditional Chinese wrestling. His classes are intense but rewarding — students say one month with him feels like three months elsewhere. Perfect for those who want real fighting skills.

🧘

Master Zhang Hui

Tai Chi & Qigong · 25 Years

A healer as much as a fighter. Master Zhang began studying Tai Chi at age 12 under his grandfather. His morning Qigong sessions by Song Mountain are legendary among students. Teaches Yang-style Tai Chi, Ba Duan Jin, and medical Qigong. Fluent in English.

🎯

Master Wang Tao

Weapons Specialist · Performance Coach

If it has a blade or a staff, Master Wang has mastered it. Specializes in Shaolin staff, straight sword, and spear. Former head of performance choreography for the Zhengzhou International Martial Arts Festival. His weapon forms are breathtaking to watch.
